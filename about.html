Professional Profile

I am a PhD student in Mechanical Engineering at The University of Alabama. My work brings together molecular simulation, machine learning, and materials engineering to understand complex material behavior and support data-driven design.

My current research focuses on functional membranes for rare-earth separation, with particular attention to membrane nanostructure and ion dynamics. I use computational tools including GROMACS, Gaussian, density functional theory methods, machine-learning interatomic potentials, and physics-informed machine learning.

Before beginning my PhD, I completed a Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology. My earlier research covered machine learning for aluminum alloys, multi-fidelity modeling of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V, computational heat transfer, finite element analysis, and sensor-based experimental design.
