Professional Profile

I am a PhD student in Mechanical Engineering at The University of Alabama. My research brings together molecular simulation, machine learning, and materials engineering to understand complex material behavior and support data-driven design.

My current work focuses on the nanostructure and ion dynamics of functional membranes for rare-earth separation. My broader interests include machine-learning interatomic potentials, physics-informed machine learning, uncertainty quantification, additive manufacturing, and computational modeling.
