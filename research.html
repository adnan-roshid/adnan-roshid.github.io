Research

Research themes connecting molecular-scale simulation, materials engineering, and machine learning.

Functional Membranes and Rare-Earth Separation

Studying membrane nanostructure and ion dynamics using molecular simulation and machine learning.

Methods: GROMACS, Gaussian, DFT, MLIP, and physics-informed machine learning.

Machine Learning for Materials

Developing data-driven models for material-property prediction, process-structure-property relationships, and uncertainty-aware analysis.

Topics: multi-fidelity neural networks, supervised learning, inverse modeling, and uncertainty quantification.

Additive Manufacturing

Modeling mechanical properties of powder-bed-fused Ti-6Al-4V using process parameters and available material-property data.

Computational Mechanics

Using finite element methods and computational fluid dynamics to investigate structures, manufacturing systems, and thermal-fluid performance.

Aluminum Alloy Informatics

Analyzing the effects of alloy composition and tempering processes on thermal conductivity with machine learning.
